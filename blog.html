Swirl Marks: The Silent Paint Killer You're Probably Causing Yourself

That spider-web pattern you see on your bonnet in direct sunlight? Those are swirl marks — and 90% of car owners create them unknowingly through incorrect washing technique. In this guide we cover how they form, why dark colours show them worst, and how our multi-stage paint correction process removes them permanently.

Quick Check: Shine a torch or phone flashlight at a 45° angle to your bonnet in a dark garage. If you see circular scratches, you have swirl damage.
